The moment

Not a business run by AI. A business that runs better because of it.

Most teams have started using AI already, in their own way and on their own initiative, and most of the people running those businesses carry a quiet suspicion that everyone else is further ahead. The honest position is simpler. The business itself is unchanged, with the same headcount, the same margin and the same processes, and that is exactly where the gain is waiting.

The aim is a more effective business, measured in time and in cost: fewer hours on work that repeats, a lower cost for each job done, and the same people spending more of their day on the work they are good at. AI is the means, not the point. It is used where the figure justifies it, built into the way the work is done, and left out where a person’s judgement is the service.

    How we work

    Four clear stages.

    Nothing is built before you have seen the figure for it. Where we can, we work with the systems you already have; where one would need to change, you hear it from us before anything starts.

    1. 1

      Chat

      Your free scoping call: twenty minutes, no charge

      A short conversation about how your business runs and where the days go. We tell you where the opportunities are likely to be and whether an assessment would be worth your money. If it would not, we say so, and you have spent twenty minutes and nothing else.

      A clear answer on whether this is right for you
    2. 2

      Assess

      Ten working days, one fixed fee

      We immerse ourselves in your business to understand how it really operates day to day: how customers arrive, how work is delivered, where it slows, and where the hours leak away without anyone quite noticing. Every recurring task is measured, the bottlenecks and roadblocks are named, and the cost of the current way of working is set out in pounds. The report highlights where the gaps are, where AI can practically help, and what should be left exactly as it is.

      A costed, prioritised plan for the best version of your business
    3. 3

      Build

      A few weeks per improvement

      We build each improvement one at a time, so the first time your team see it, it already works. They are shown how it works, what it does not do, and who to call. It is built around your existing systems wherever possible, measured against the figure in the report, and every record it touches remains yours, with full access.

      A proven improvement your team understands
    4. 4

      Embed

      Ongoing, month by month

      The improvement becomes part of how the business runs, and stops needing your attention. We host and monitor it, resolve faults by the next working day, and continue to improve it as the business changes. Each month you receive a single page showing what it delivered, in hours and in pounds, measured the same way as at the outset. There is no lock-in: thirty days’ notice ends the arrangement, your data always stays with you, and after a year the system itself is yours to keep.

      Reliable, measured, and yours

    Our story

    Inspired to help other businesses.

    Tomoni AI is an AI consultancy that grew out of running businesses rather than advising them. Tom Williams has run his own businesses for more than ten years, currently a training provider and a product brand, and for all of that time carried the administrative load every business carries: the work done by hand, after the work that earns the money, in the hours that were meant for something else.

    The first systems were built for those businesses. Enrolments that create their own records. A weekly summary of what is owed and what is due. An inbox that sorts itself before anyone opens it. They removed the repetitive work and, more importantly, they were still doing so months later without anyone’s attention.

    Tomoni brings the same approach to other businesses, whatever their size or sector: the admin and repetitive work automated one improvement at a time, with the figure established before anything is built. The name is Japanese, 共に, meaning together. It describes the arrangement. We work alongside the people who run the business.
